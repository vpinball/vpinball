# Visual Pinball Privacy Policy

This policy applies to the Visual Pinball mobile apps for iOS, Android and Meta Quest.

Last updated: October 3, 2026

## Data collection

The apps do not collect, store, transmit or share any personal information.

## Data on your device

Tables, settings and other files you import stay on your device. On iOS they are also visible in the Files app and through Finder; on Android you may choose a folder of your own for them. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

## Network use

The apps only use the network when you ask them to:

- The optional built-in web server lets a browser on your local network manage your table files. It is off by default and only reachable on that network.
- External DMD devices (DMDServer, ZeDMD WiFi) are contacted only if you configure one in Settings.
- Links to the project website, source code and Discord open in your browser or mail app, which have their own privacy policies.
